Privacy Policy

Punch & Charms / Пунш и Чары · Android
Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy describes data processing in Punch & Charms (Russian name: Пунш и Чары; package com.punchandcharms.game). The developer and privacy contact is HedraGame, hedraxgame@gmail.com.

1. Game data on your device

No account is required. Completed levels, unfinished puzzles, game coins and hearts, game reward history, language, audio and vibration preferences, and ad frequency counters are saved locally on your device. The developer does not operate a server receiving these saves and does not provide cloud synchronization of game progress.

The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise or approximate device location. This version has no real-money payments and does not collect payment details.

2. Advertising and third-party SDK

The game uses Yandex Mobile Ads SDK for interstitial ads and optional ads that provide game rewards. When ads load or display, the SDK communicates with the advertising provider's servers. Depending on identifier availability and device settings, data processed may include advertising and other technical identifiers, IP address, device and operating system information, and information about ad requests and impressions. This data is used to deliver advertising and measure its operation.

In the current version, the game passes a lack of consent for personalization to the SDK and disables location tracking and additional app analytics reporting through the advertising SDK. This does not mean that no technical data is processed when an ad is requested. The game does not send its local puzzle save to the ad provider as a separate gameplay database.

Advertising data may be transmitted to Yandex and providers involved in ad delivery. Their processing rules, retention practices and contact options are described in the Yandex Privacy Policy. Information about Mobile Ads SDK data is available in the provider's documentation. According to the SDK provider, transmission of the user data listed in its guidance is protected using HTTPS/TLS.

You can restrict or delete your advertising ID in Android privacy and advertising settings where supported. Puzzles remain playable without internet access, but ads and rewards tied to viewing them may be unavailable.

3. Notifications

Heart-restoration and return reminders are generated locally on your device. Reminder times, language and heart state are stored for this purpose. These reminders do not require sending contacts or a remote push notification token to a developer-operated server.

On supported Android versions, the game requests notification permission through the operating system. You can disable permission and notifications in system app settings. Delivery depends on Android restrictions and device power-saving settings.

4. Support requests

If you choose to email support, HedraGame receives your sender address, message and any attachments you provide. These are used to reply and address your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

5. Retention and deletion

Local saves and preferences remain until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Clearing data through Android settings resets game progress. Uninstalling the game is not a request to delete data already processed by the ad provider.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Contact the address below to ask about access, correction or deletion of information held by HedraGame. Requests concerning Yandex data may also be sent through the channels listed in its policy; HedraGame does not promise to independently delete data controlled by the ad provider.

6. Additional information

The game does not ask for your age. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support without any required parent or guardian permission, contact us so we can review a deletion request. The game's age rating is listed separately on its store page.

If data practices change, this page will be updated with a new revision date. This policy applies to the game; the website, app store and external pages are also subject to their respective providers' policies.
